Our Story

Every love story is beautiful, but ours is ten years in the making.

CHAPTER ONE

A Friend Request Changed Everything

Back in 2016, Ian was simply scrolling through Facebook hoping to meet someone special. One friend request was accepted. One simple conversation began. Neither of them knew that this small moment would become the beginning of a lifetime together.

CHAPTER TWO

Officially Us

On June 26, 2016, they officially became a couple. What started with simple conversations slowly grew into a relationship built on trust, friendship, and love.

CHAPTER THREE

The Little Things

Their happiest memories weren't expensive vacations or grand celebrations. They were jeepney rides after school, tusok-tusok dates, laughing together, and walking home while trying not to get caught. Those simple moments became their greatest treasures.

CHAPTER FOUR

Through Every Challenge

Every relationship has difficult seasons. A misunderstanding once kept them apart for almost a month. But love isn't about never falling. It's about choosing each other every single time.

CHAPTER FIVE

The Proposal

July 24, 2025. During a trip to Taiwan, Ian surprised Desiree with a proposal inside the Maokong Gondola. Thinking the ride was ending, he hurriedly asked the question that would change both of their lives forever. She said "Yes."

CHAPTER SIX

Forever Begins Here

Ten years after a simple Facebook friend request, they are finally preparing for the next chapter of their lives together. Their greatest adventure is only beginning.
